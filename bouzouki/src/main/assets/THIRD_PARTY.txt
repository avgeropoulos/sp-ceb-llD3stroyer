basic_pitch.onnx is the Basic Pitch note transcription model (icassp_2022 nmp.onnx)
by Spotify, https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch, licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
basic_pitch.tflite is the same model as Spotify publishes it for TensorFlow Lite (icassp_2022 nmp.tflite), Apache License 2.0.

The instrument separation model (Demucs v4 htdemucs_6s by Meta, https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs,
MIT License), exported to ONNX by tools/demucs in this repository, is downloaded by the app from its model
release on first use; it is not included in the app.

alphaTab (https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab) is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0; its Bravura
font under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; alphaSkia under the BSD 3-Clause License.

The fingering engine's tuttut mode is ported from tuttut (https://github.com/natecdr/tuttut), Copyright (c) 2023
Nathan Candre, MIT License.
