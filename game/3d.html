Backyard Boop 3Dchase cam, starring Hercules & Apollo

Run around the yard right behind your dog. Sneak up on squirrels and bunnies and give them a friendly boop. Every 3 boops calls in the Giant Golden Goose. Use the minimap in the corner to find the critters hiding behind you.

🐿️Squirrel 10 pts. Fast and zig-zaggy.
🐰Bunny 15 pts. Hops in big bursts.
🪿Giant Goose 100+ pts. Appears after 3 boops.
💦Sprinklers & Mr. Bubbles the rolling bathtub. 3 splashes means bath time!
🟤Mud slows you down.
🦴Treats fall from the sky. Grab one for a 7-second boost.

🎮 Controller ready! Left stick or D-pad: run. Right stick: look around. A (or any face button): power. Start: pause. Press left or right to pick a dog, then A.

Run with WASD or the arrow keys: your dog runs the way you point and the camera swings round behind him. Q and E turn the camera. Space uses your power: Hercules zooms so fast he stays dry, Apollo leaps over mud, spray and bathtubs. On a phone or tablet, slide your thumb on the left side to run and drag on the right side to look around. A quick tap or the round button uses the power. Game controllers work too. P pauses.
