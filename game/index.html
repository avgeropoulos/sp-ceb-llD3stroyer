Backyard Boopstarring Hercules & Apollo

Squirrels and bunnies are loose in the yard. Run over and give them a friendly boop on the nose. Every 3 boops calls in the Giant Golden Goose for big bonus points.

🐿️Squirrel 10 pts. Fast and zig-zaggy.
🐰Bunny 15 pts. Hops in big bursts.
🪿Giant Goose 100+ pts. Appears after 3 boops.
💦Sprinklers & Mr. Bubbles the rolling bathtub. 3 splashes means bath time!
🟤Mud slows you down.
⏱️Reach the goal before the clock runs out, or it's nap time.

Move with WASD or arrow keys, or touch and drag. In team mode Hercules uses WASD, Apollo uses the arrows, or each player drags a dog. P pauses.
